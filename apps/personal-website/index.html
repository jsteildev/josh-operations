My story

A little about me

I grew up in a divorced family, which shaped how I see the world in ways I'm still putting into words. I studied electrical engineering at UConn, then went to work at Lockheed Martin in their missile defense division. Engineering taught me how to take complex systems apart and rebuild them better, and that way of thinking followed me out of the lab and into business.

Today I live in Austin, Texas, where I co-founded The Credit Brothers with my brother Chris. We help people repair their credit through a system grounded in consumer law, and I'm proud of the work we do for our clients every single day.

Outside of work, I'm a points and travel enthusiast. There's nothing I love more than a well planned trip, a great meal in a new city, and coming home to the people who matter most. My family is everything to me: my parents, and my brother Chris, my partner in business and in life.

I try to take care of myself the same way I take care of the people around me. Training, good sleep, breathwork and meditation keep me grounded. Fitness is a daily practice for me, not a phase, and the quieter practices matter just as much as the loud ones.

The business

The Credit Brothers

My brother Chris and I run The Credit Brothers, a consumer law focused credit repair company. We built a dispute system rooted in consumer protection law, and this year it has helped pay our clients over $100k in settlements.

Now we're packaging that system so other businesses can offer credit repair inside their own suite of services, without needing to hire a full staff to run it.
